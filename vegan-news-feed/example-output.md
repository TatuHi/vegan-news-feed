Note two things visible in this specific run that are worth pointing out for anyone comparing it against `SKILL.md`:

- Every source here happened to be English-language (flagged with 🇬🇧 next to each source, per the rule in step 3). At the time this was written, that looked like ordinary day-to-day variance — Finnish sources (`animalia.fi`, `vegaaniliitto.fi`, the Finnish Google News queries) are checked every run, they just didn't turn up anything this particular day. It later turned out to be more than variance: `PROCESS.md`'s iterations 9-12 trace a real, verified bug in how the Finnish Google News queries worked (result ranking, not date filtering) plus missing search terms, found via `vegan-news-feed-review`'s analysis and a user-reported missed article, and fixed. Left here uncorrected-in-place deliberately, as an honest snapshot of what looked normal before the bug was found — not retouched with hindsight.
- All 9 links are direct publisher URLs, not Google News redirect links — several of these stories were originally surfaced via Google News with only a headline-echo `summary`, then enriched and resolved to their real source per step 3's rules before being included here.
